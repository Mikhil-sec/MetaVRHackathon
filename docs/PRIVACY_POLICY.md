# RICOCHET Privacy Policy

*Effective date: [DATE]. Contact: [SUPPORT EMAIL]*

RICOCHET is a mixed reality game for Meta Quest headsets. This policy explains what information the game uses and where it goes. In short: the game runs on your headset, keeps your progress on your headset, and sends nothing to us.

## What the game uses, and where it stays

**Your room (scene data).** With your permission, the game reads the room layout you created in Space Setup (walls, floor, ceiling, furniture outlines) to build each game board around your real surfaces. This data is processed on your headset only. We never receive it, store it elsewhere or share it.

**Hand tracking.** Your hand positions are used in real time to let you pinch, pull and release. They are not recorded, stored or transmitted.

**Eye tracking (Quest Pro only, optional).** If your headset supports it and you allow it, the direction you look is used in real time to aim and to choose rewards. Eye tracking data is not recorded, stored or transmitted. You can decline it and play with head direction instead.

**Spatial anchors (trophies).** When you win a run, a trophy is placed on your wall and saved as a spatial anchor so it reappears in the same place. Anchors are stored on your headset by the Meta system software.

**Game progress.** Your current run, trophies, scores and settings are saved in the game's storage on your headset. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

## What leaves your headset

**Leaderboards.** If leaderboards are available, your score is submitted through Meta's Platform SDK, and the leaderboard shows it with your Meta Horizon username. This is handled by Meta under the [Meta Privacy Policy](https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/).

**Engine statistics.** The Unity engine the game is built with may send anonymous technical information (such as the device model and operating system version) to Unity Technologies. See the [Unity Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy).

That is all. RICOCHET has no accounts of its own, no ads, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no chat and no purchases, and we do not sell or share any information.

## Meta's platform

Your Meta Quest headset and your Meta account are governed by Meta's own terms and privacy policy, which apply to all apps on the platform.

## Children

RICOCHET is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
